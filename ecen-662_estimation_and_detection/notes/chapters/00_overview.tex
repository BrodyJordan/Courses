\chapter{Overview}

Suppose that the distribution of random variable $X \in Xc$, is determined by a parameter $\theta \in \Theta$:
\[
    X \sim f_\theta(x)
\]
Here, the functional form of $f_\theta$ is known, but the value of $\theta$ is unknown. We use $f_\theta$ to denote probability density function (pdf) and $p_\theta$ to denote probability mass function (pmf), and $f_\theta$ for generic descriptions.

In statistical inference, we observe a realization $x$ of $X$ and need to answer some questions about $\theta$, such as:
\begin{itemize}
    \setlength\itemsep{-2.5pt}
    \item Estimation: what is a good guess of $\theta?$
    \item Detection: is $\theta \in \Theta_1$, or is $\theta \in \Theta_2$?
\end{itemize}
